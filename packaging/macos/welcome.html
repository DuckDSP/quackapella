Quackapella, by DuckDSP

Real-time vocal manipulation: autotune, natural pitch shifting with formant preservation, MIDI harmonizer and vocoder.

With its companion Quackapella Link: put on another track, it sends the rest of the song to the mixing assistant, or a synth to the vocoder, without any side-chain routing.

Installed formats: Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand), VST3 and CLAP. You can untick the ones you don't need in the "Customize" step.

After installing, restart your audio software so that it finds the plugins.
